Questions

Everything you need to know.

A simple platform should make the important things easy to understand.

Is Lambadina free for job seekers?

Yes. The platform is designed to help job seekers discover vacancy information without a paywall.

Where do vacancy details come from?

Each listing contains the source/application information available in the vacancy record. Applicants should review the original application route before submitting documents.

Can employers work with Lambadina?

Yes. Employers can contact the platform about publishing and promoting opportunities.

Can I follow new vacancies on Telegram?

Yes. Lambadina's Telegram channel can be used for vacancy alerts and updates.
