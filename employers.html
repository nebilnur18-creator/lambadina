Common questions

Employer FAQ

How long does a listing stay live?+

Until the deadline you provide passes. You can also ask us to take it down earlier if the position is filled.

Can I update a listing after it's posted?+

Yes — just message us with the changes and we'll update it.

Do you write the listing for us?+

We format whatever details you send into Lambadina's standard listing style, so it reads clearly and consistently alongside every other opportunity on the platform.

Reach out first — we'll confirm the right plan for your opportunity and share payment details directly.
